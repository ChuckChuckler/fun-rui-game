# MY REALLY FUN PHYSICS STUDYING GAME FT. RUI

Tsukasa fails his test. Rui agrees to help him study!! Will you get all the questions right and be the absolute alpha gigachad of physics? Or will you get too many wrong and be forced to try out Rui's new human cannon...?

this game got me a 100 on my quiz so you KNOW it's good

## How to Play
you should totally play this with sound on, trust me
You can skip the story at the beginning by clicking the "I don't care" button. Click anywhere on the screen to progress through the short VN.

Answer the question on the board behind Rui! Your solution should be rounded to at the very least the nearest hundredth. If you're correct, you'll get confetti, a celebratory sound effect, and a fun and happy reaction image! If you're wrong, you'll get no confetti and a disappointed sound, but you'll still get a fun reaction image.

Keep track of the meter at the bottom of the screen. If Tsukasa's head gets too close to the cannon on the red side, you'll lose! If he gets close to the green side, that means you're getting closer to winning! Win by successfully reaching the green end of the meter. You can try again if you lose.

## Topics Covered
Springs:
    - Finding the mass of a weight attached to a spring
    - Finding the spring constant of a spring
    - Finding the stretch length/displacement of a spring
    - Solving for potential energy
    - Solving for force exerted
    - Solving for the height of an object propelled by a spring
    - Solving for the velocity of an object propelled by a spring

Pendulums:
    - Finding the length of a pendulum
    - Findng the g of another planet
    - Calculating period
    - Calculating frequency
    - Solving for the number of seconds in which a certain number of cycles is made
    - Solving for the number of cycles within a certain number of seconds

Notes:
    a. These are very basic problems
    b. The values used in the calculations are determined through rng, so they may not be entirely possible in reality
